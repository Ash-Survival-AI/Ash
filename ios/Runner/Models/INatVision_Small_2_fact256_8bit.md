# INatVision Small Model

Source: https://github.com/inaturalist/model-files/releases/tag/v25.01.15

File: `INatVision_Small_2_fact256_8bit.mlmodel`

License: MIT, from the iNaturalist `model-files` repository:
https://github.com/inaturalist/model-files/blob/main/LICENSE

Ash uses this model only as a limited candidate-label signal inside Mushroom
Safety mode. The public small model is not an edible/poisonous mushroom
classifier and does not cover most mushroom species.
